Overview and notation
=====================

Three texts serve different purposes.

* The **focused manuscript** proves the compact trace-sparsity theorem and is
  the shortest route through the argument.
* The **companion manuscript** develops the wider algebraic-group and Kummer
  viewpoint, including the projective square-root map, inner and outer forms,
  auxiliary theorems, and approaches that are not needed for the focused
  proof.
* The **positive-trace-gap manuscript**, by Nikolay Bogachev, proves the
  positive-gap characterization and supplies the square-root/commensurator
  starting point for the broader project.

The :doc:`manuscripts` page gives direct links to all three PDFs and their
TeX sources.

Basic objects
-------------

Let :math:`S` be a closed orientable hyperbolic surface, let
:math:`\Pi=\pi_1(S)`, and let
:math:`x` be a faithful cocompact Fuchsian character.  Its image is a lattice
:math:`\Gamma<\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb R)`.  Write
:math:`\widetilde\Gamma<\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)` for the full inverse image.
The signed trace set is

.. math::

   T(\Gamma)=\{\operatorname{Tr}g:g\in\widetilde\Gamma\}.

It is a set of values: multiplicities are discarded.  Because both lifts of
a projective element occur, :math:`T(\Gamma)=-T(\Gamma)`.

The subgroup generated by squares is

.. math::

   \Pi^{(2)}=\langle\gamma^2:\gamma\in\Pi\rangle.

It has finite index because
:math:`\Pi/\Pi^{(2)}\cong H_1(S,\mathbb F_2)`.  Two lifts of a projective
representation differ by a sign character, which is trivial on
:math:`\Pi^{(2)}`.  Traces on this subgroup therefore have no lift ambiguity.

Trace and length
----------------

For a nonidentity hyperbolic element,

.. math::

   |\operatorname{Tr}g|=2\cosh\!\left(\frac{\ell(g)}2\right).

Thus trace height :math:`X` corresponds to length
:math:`2\log X+O(1)`.  This is the dictionary between the trace-counting
statements used here and their customary length-spectrum formulations.

Arithmetic lattices
-------------------

In the Fuchsian setting, an admissible quaternion algebra :math:`B/k` is
defined over a totally real number field, is split at one distinguished real
place, and is ramified at every other real place.  The norm-one group of an
order in :math:`B`, mapped through the distinguished splitting, gives a
lattice in :math:`\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb R)`.  A Fuchsian group is arithmetic
when it is commensurable with such a lattice.

The final proof does not assume a quaternion algebra in advance.  It first
forces trace integrality and bounded nondistinguished conjugates; Takeuchi's
criterion then supplies arithmeticity.
